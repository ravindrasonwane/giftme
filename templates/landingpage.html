Questions before you create?

What exactly am I making?

A small private web page for one person. On their phone they tap through it: a bow to pull, a door to knock on, a puzzle to solve. You add the names, photos and words; we give you the link.

Do they need to download an app?

No. It opens in any phone browser, with no account for you or for them.

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. You watch the whole gift free first. Check the spelling of their name there, because the gift is sealed when you pay.

How much does it cost?

₹99 per gift, paid once by UPI. No subscription.

Who else can see our photos?

Only people who have the link. There is no public gallery, and we don’t post your gift anywhere.

How long does the link last?

90 days, and they can open it as often as they like. After that it is deleted for good, photos included.

I paid but have no link

Don’t pay again. Open the same gift page in the same browser you paid from and it picks up your payment. Still stuck? WhatsApp us or email support@giftpixel.in with your UPI reference.
